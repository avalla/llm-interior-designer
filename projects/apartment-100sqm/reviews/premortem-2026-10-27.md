# Pre-mortem - Apartment 100 m2

Reviewer who did not design the layout. Sources: `brief.md`, `output/Apartment_Plan.png`, `output/verification_report.md`; scenarios from `docs/method.md` par. 8, patterns from `docs/patterns.md`, thresholds from `docs/design-rules.md`. No model file was opened and no code was run.

Coordinate convention used below: report positions are the minimum corner of each footprint in metres, origin at the south-west exterior corner, y = 0 on the south (glazed) side, interior faces at x 0.30 / 12.20 and y 0.30 / 7.70. This reading reproduces the report's own clearances (island / tall units 1.75 - 0.90 = 85 cm, island / base units 7.05 - 6.10 = 95 cm, Chair_4 seat 7.70 - 6.65 = 105 cm), so the derived measures are consistent with it. Measures marked "read on the plan" are scaled from the PNG (about 118 px/m) and carry a +/- 3 cm uncertainty.

It is six months later. Two adults live here (brief, assumption 4). These are the stories of what went wrong.

## Failure stories

**S01 - Living area (entrance to kitchen) - carrying the shopping to the fridge - significant.** Scenario: coming in with shopping. The occupant enters through the armored door (x 6.75-7.80, north wall) with four bags, heading for the fridge in the tall units on the far west wall (x 0.30-0.90). The obvious line along the north wall passes behind the north dining chair, but Chair_4 (y 6.64-7.07) leaves only 63 cm to the north wall (7.70 - 7.07), below the 80 cm main passage, and nothing at all once someone sits there. So the shopping goes the long way: south round the table, past the stools, round the island. The report confirms it: kitchen sink 9.1 m and fridge/oven 10.2 m "from the entrance" for a straight-line distance of about 5.5-6.5 m. Six months in, the bags end up on the dining table every time. Pattern broken: P11 Sitting circle (seats out of the main route): the dining table sits on the main route between the front door and the kitchen. Also method par. 5 (shopping needs a practical route).

**S02 - Living area (entrance) - nowhere to put things down - significant.** Scenario: coming in with shopping or luggage. There is no console, shelf or bench by the door: the only entrance storage is Entrance_Wardrobe (100 x 45 x 240), with its front 2.40 m from the door threshold (y 7.70 to 5.30), route 1.9 m in the report. One metre of wardrobe has to hold coats, shoes, bags, umbrellas and keys for two adults all year. Keys and post land on the dining table, which is 1.6 m from the entrance ("table east side" route). Pattern broken: P03 Entrance room (storage and a landing surface within two steps of the front door). The report's P03 check only proves the wardrobe can be reached, not that there is a landing surface.

**S03 - Living area (entrance / dining) - hanging a coat while someone is at the table - significant.** Scenario: table occupied while others pass to other rooms. One partner sits at the east chair (Chair_2, x 5.89-6.32, y 5.89-6.31); seated and pushed back, the chair reaches about x 6.60. The other comes home, opens the entrance wardrobe (x 6.95-7.95, front at y 5.30, two 50 cm leaves swinging north, 70 cm user zone up to y 6.00) and stands there. The gap between the occupied chair and the person at the wardrobe is about 35 cm (6.95 - 6.60), and that pinch is the only way from the door to the night-zone sliding door. With nobody seated, the same pinch is 86 cm (report, below the 90 cm comfort target); the Entrance_Mirror (x 7.93, y 5.85-6.65) adds a third person standing in the same strip. Patterns broken: P10 Eating atmosphere (table without walls or furniture around it, standing in the arrival zone) and P03 (entrance storage placed on the through route).

**S04 - Living area (entrance) - arriving on top of the dining table - improvement.** Scenario: coming in with luggage; guests arriving. R03 asked for an entrance open to the living area and the plan delivers it literally: the door opens onto the table, 1.6 m away, with nothing marking a threshold (no change of floor, ceiling or light; the wardrobe faces the door instead of screening the room). Whoever sits at the table gets the draught and the view of the landing every time the door opens. Pattern broken: P01 Entrance transition. The compromise between R03 and P01 is not stated anywhere.

**S05 - Living area (dining / kitchen) - one diner closes the kitchen - significant.** Scenario: table occupied while others pass; two people in the kitchen. The west chair (Chair_1, x 4.38-4.81) has 105 cm from the table edge (x 4.80) to the island's east end (x 3.75). A seated, pushed-back diner leaves about 25-35 cm, which closes the gap between the island and the table. The kitchen can then only be entered round the island's west end, through the 85 cm aisle in front of the fridge/oven column (report: island / tall units 85 cm, below the 90 cm comfort target). If the cook stands at the open fridge there, the kitchen is closed. Pattern broken: P11 Sitting circle (seats out of the main route).

**S06 - Living area (dining) - six people for dinner - improvement.** Scenario: extended table. The table is a fixed 110 cm round with four chairs, under a fixed pendant (Pendant_Table). Two adults can host only two guests. Extending west closes the island passage (S05), extending north closes the 63 cm passage at the wall (S01), and extending east grows into the entrance pinch (S03). There is no stated alternative (extendable table, folding chairs, storage for them). Pattern broken: P10 Eating atmosphere (dining place sized for occupants and guests, method par. 7).

**S07 - Living area (kitchen) - opening the fridge or the oven with someone passing - significant.** Scenario: two people in the kitchen. The fridge and oven are inside Tall_Units_Fridge_Oven (60 x 270 x 230, west wall); the report lists their footprint and their tilt-up check, but no leaf for the fridge door or the oven door. The aisle opposite the island's west end is 85 cm. A 60 cm fridge door open at 90 degrees leaves about 25 cm, and a dropped oven door (about 50-55 cm) leaves about 30 cm, with the user standing inside that swing. Because of S05, this aisle is also the fallback route into the kitchen. No pattern in P01-P18 covers appliance doors; rule broken: method par. 7 ("fridge, oven, dishwasher and corner units open with a person in front"). R02 is therefore not proven for appliance doors.

**S08 - Living area (kitchen) - loading the dishwasher - significant.** Scenario: loading the dishwasher; two people in the kitchen. There is no dishwasher in the furniture schedule or on the plan; the 330 cm base run holds a sink (x 1.90-2.70) and a hob (x 3.35-3.95). Wherever it goes, its door drops about 60 cm into the 95 cm aisle between base units and island (report: below the 110 cm comfort target), leaving about 35 cm. That cuts the kitchen in two, fridge side and hob side, for as long as it is being loaded. Even without the dishwasher, two people working back to back (one at the sink, one prepping on the island) share 95 cm with about 45-50 cm of body each. Bins and recycling have no place on the plan either. No pattern in P01-P18 applies; rule broken: method par. 5 and par. 7 (store-wash-prepare-cook-clean-up sequence; appliances open with a person in front).

**S09 - Living area (kitchen) - pan handles over the passage - significant.** Scenario: cooking while others pass. The hob ends at x 3.95 and the base run ends at x 4.20: only 25 cm of landing space to its right. That is too little for a pot lid or a hot pan, and the end of the run opens straight onto the passage towards the dining chairs (Chair_4 at x 5.14). Hot pans go down on the island across the 95 cm aisle, i.e. carried over the route. No pattern in P01-P18 applies; rule broken: method par. 7 (landing space next to appliances) and par. 1 (safety first).

**S10 - Living area (kitchen) - opening the kitchen window while working at the counter - significant.** Scenario: opening windows. Window_Kitchen (180 x 100, sill 1.20, two inward leaves of about 90 cm) sits above a 65 cm deep counter. At 90 degrees the leaves stand out to about y 6.87 (arc read on the plan), about 18 cm past the counter front (y 7.05), at 1.20-2.20 m height: head and face height of whoever stands at the sink. The right leaf, hinged at x 3.25, stands about 10 cm from the hob's west edge (x 3.35), next to the flames whenever the window is open for cooking. The report checks the leaves only against objects (tap, wall units), not against the person at the counter. No pattern in P01-P18 applies; rule broken: method par. 7 ("sink, tap and window without interference") and design-rules "inward window leaf" (lesson L07).

**S11 - Living area (kitchen) - the corner of the fridge column no one can open - improvement.** Scenario: cleaning and maintenance. Tall_Units_Fridge_Oven runs from y 5.00 to 7.70 and Base_Units starts at x 0.90, exactly against its front. The last 65 cm of the tall column (y 7.05-7.70) has the base run in front of it: about 0.9 m3 of tall storage that cannot be opened (or a hinged door that hits the worktop end). Rule broken: design-rules "Corner between two L-shaped wardrobes / units: corner unit or no hinged leaves in the corner" (lesson L01); no pattern in P01-P18 covers it directly.

**S12 - Living area (lounge) - getting to the sofa - significant.** Scenario: cleaning; carrying a tray to the sofa; guests. The report gives the route to the sofa as 12.1 m and to the armchair as 11.5 m from the entrance, for about 7 m in a straight line. The plan explains it: the chaise (about x 2.60-3.40, y 2.97-3.80) and the coffee table (x 1.90-2.60, y 1.80-2.60) are about 0.4 m apart, and the armchair (y up to 1.25) and the coffee table are 55 cm apart; both are below the 60 cm route. The only usable entry to the seats is the 70 cm strip between the sofa's south arm (y 1.00) and the 4.5 m glazing (y 0.30), reached by going round the sofa back past the bookcase. From the dining side, the other approach runs behind the stools: 86 cm to the chaise back with the stools tucked in, about 50-55 cm with three people at the island. The robot vacuum and the mop face the same pocket. Pattern broken: P11 Sitting circle (seats reachable from outside the main route, without threading along the window) and method par. 9 (routes that exist only by moving furniture).

**S13 - Living area (lounge) - a cup, a lamp and the TV from the sofa - improvement.** Scenario: sitting, reading, watching TV. The main seat front of the L sofa is at about x 3.40 (read on the plan), and the coffee table's near edge is at x 2.60: about 80 cm, out of arm's reach, so only the chaise reaches it. There is no lamp registered at the sofa (Floor_Lamp is at the armchair, x 0.85-1.25). The armchair by the window sits about 48 degrees off the TV axis at about 2.1 m, so it does not join the viewing group. Patterns broken: P15 Reading light with its seat and P14 Pools of light (sofa), P11 Sitting circle (armchair).

**S14 - Living area (south glazing) - curtains, blinds and opening the big window - significant.** Scenario: opening windows and blinds; cleaning. The south French window is 4.5 m of floor-level glass (net x 1.00-5.50) facing full sun. The armchair stands 12 cm from the glass (y 0.42), the floor lamp in front of its west end (x 0.85-1.25, y 0.50), and the sofa's south arm 70 cm away along x 2.60-4.50. A curtain track needs about 15-20 cm and a stacking zone at the ends; at the west end there is none. Opening or cleaning the western sliding panel means moving the armchair and the lamp. The 70 cm strip in front of the remaining glass is also the only access to the sofa (S12). Solar shading is NOT VERIFIABLE in the report (lighting/thermal not modelled), yet this is the room's only sun control. Pattern broken: P08 Light on two sides ("never block a window with furniture").

**S15 - Living area / night hall - closing the night-zone door at night - improvement.** Scenario: night route; leaving. Plant (70 x 70 x 130, x 5.50-6.20, y 3.00-3.70) stands 25 cm from the partition, exactly where Door_Night_Zone (surface-mounted sliding, living side) parks when open (leaf at y about 2.68-3.69, read on the plan), and 5 cm into the door's own span (opening y 3.65-4.75). Every evening the door is closed by reaching past 1.3 m of foliage to the leaf's leading edge, and the plant sits in front of the doorway's south jamb. The report only checks that the open leaf hits nothing. No pattern in P01-P18 applies; rule broken: method par. 9 (routes and openings that work only by omitting the person using them).

**S16 - Bedroom / living area - hearing the dinner party - improvement.** Scenario: one partner sleeps while the other hosts. The bedroom's west wall is a 10 cm partition (x 6.45-6.55) with the dining table 55 cm away (table east edge x 5.90). The night-zone sliding door runs on a surface-mounted track fixed to the same partition, so every opening and closing rolls along the bedroom wall. Only the bookcase (y 0.60-2.60) buffers part of that wall; the dining stretch has nothing. Acoustics are NOT VERIFIABLE in the report. Pattern broken: P13 Closets between rooms (storage as an acoustic buffer between bedroom and noisy rooms).

**S17 - Night hall / bathroom - the guest who needs the toilet - significant.** Scenario: table occupied while others pass; night route. There is one bathroom and it is inside the night zone. R04 closes that zone with a door, so every guest slides it open, enters the night hall and passes the bedroom door (x 6.72-7.57, 17 cm from the night-zone door) to reach the WC. The report's privacy checks only verify that the Bedroom room itself is not crossed; they do not test the night zone. The same single WC serves both adults at 7:30 while one of them showers, and a guest staying overnight sleeps on a sofa with no door and walks through the night zone at night. Method par. 5 asks to state the single-bathroom compromise; the brief and the report do not. Pattern broken: P02 Intimacy gradient.

**S18 - Night hall - a dark tube - significant.** Scenario: entering, moving, cleaning. The night hall is 5.65 m long by 1.10 m (6.2 m2), with no window, five doors and openings (night zone, bedroom, bathroom, closet, utility cupboard), and its only borrowed light comes through the night-zone door, which R04 keeps closed. The stretch east of the bathroom door (x 9.10-11.75, about 2.9 m2) serves only the closet sliding door and the utility cupboard. In a 100 m2 flat with a single WC, that is the space a second WC would have needed (S17). R05 ("rooms that do not feel oppressive") is checked in the report only through floor occupancy of the habitable rooms, so this corridor was never assessed. No pattern in P01-P18 applies; rule broken: method par. 5 ("no leftover rooms or oversized corridors").

**S19 - Night hall - getting the vacuum cleaner out - improvement.** Scenario: cleaning. Utility_Cupboard (45 x 110 x 240, hall east end) has a hinged leaf of about 0.9 m (arc read on the plan) swinging across a 1.10 m corridor. It parks flat against the bedroom partition (y about 3.71), but while it swings it sweeps most of the hall width, and the user's 70 cm zone (x 11.05-11.75) overlaps the closet doorway (x 10.49-11.19). Taking out the vacuum or the ironing board blocks the walk-in closet. Pattern broken: P17 Slide in the narrow (sliding doors in corridors under 1.20 m).

**S20 - Bathroom - the shower soaks the washing machine - blocking.** Scenario: using the shower; laundry. Shower_Tray is 140 x 100 (x 8.05-9.45, y 6.70-7.70) with a single 80 cm glass (Shower_Glass, x 8.05-8.85 along y 6.70). The tray's whole east side (1.0 m) and 60 cm of its south side are open. Washing_Machine (x 9.65-10.25, y 7.10-7.70) stands 20 cm from the open east edge, about 1.1 m from the shower head (x 8.33-8.57 on the north wall). Spray and runoff reach an appliance and its socket and supply inside the shower's splash zone. The 20 cm slot between tray and machine is a permanently wet strip that cannot be mopped. Services are NOT VERIFIABLE in the report, which is why this electrical-safety conflict was never caught. No pattern in P01-P18 applies; rule broken: method par. 7 ("door, shower enclosure and units without interference") and par. 1 (safety first). The only blocking story in this list: an electrical appliance in a wet zone is a safety violation (method par. 1, safety first).

**S21 - Bathroom - opening the only window - significant.** Scenario: opening windows; ventilation after the shower. Window_Bathroom (60 x 60, sill 1.60) is directly above the washing machine (window x 9.50-10.20, machine x 9.65-10.25). Its handle, at about 1.9 m, is reached by leaning across 60 cm of machine. This 0.36 m2 window is the bathroom's only ventilation according to the report ("window or mechanical ventilation": VERIFIED by presence). A shower with an open side (S20) and a washing machine produce a lot of moisture, and a window that is hard to reach stays shut, which brings condensation and mould onto the shared partition with the windowless walk-in closet (S25). Mechanical extraction is not declared. No pattern in P01-P18 applies; rule broken: method par. 7 (ventilation; openings usable) and design-rules "Bathroom: opening window or mechanical ventilation" (satisfied on paper only).

**S22 - Bathroom - knees between WC and bidet - improvement.** Scenario: using WC and bidet. WC (y 5.97-6.33) and Bidet_Wall_Hung (y 5.20-5.56) are 41 cm apart edge to edge and 77 cm axis to axis: about 38 cm each side of each axis, below the usual 40 cm. The bidet's axis is 53 cm from the south wall. Both fit, but elbows and knees touch the neighbour fixture. No pattern in P01-P18 applies; rule broken: method par. 7 (use of washbasin, WC, bidet).

**S23 - Bathroom - where does the laundry dry, and where do the towels go? - significant.** Scenario: laundry, cleaning, maintenance. The washing machine is in the bathroom, but the plan has no balcony, no utility room and no free floor for a drying rack (about 1.8 x 0.6 m open). The bathroom's free floor between washbasin (x 8.55) and WC (x 9.70) is about 1.15 x 1.85 m, and the night hall is 1.10 m wide. The rack ends up in the living area in front of the south glazing, or in the bedroom. Bathroom storage for two adults is limited to the 50 x 80 washbasin unit. No pattern in P01-P18 applies; rule broken: method par. 5 ("shopping, laundry and cleaning have practical routes").

**S24 - Walk-in closet - the corner shelf no one reaches - improvement.** Scenario: opening every storage unit. Closet_Unit_East runs the full 285 cm (y 4.85-7.70) and Closet_Unit_North (x 10.35-11.60, y 7.10-7.70) stands in front of its last 60 cm. The north end of the east unit (60 x 60 x 240) has no front and is lost, about 20% of the east run. The report's access zones test only the open faces. Rule broken: design-rules "Corner between two L-shaped wardrobes: open walk-in or corner unit" (lesson L01); no pattern in P01-P18 covers it directly.

**S25 - Walk-in closet - clothes next to the shower - improvement.** Scenario: daily dressing; maintenance. The closet (5.3 m2) has no window and no stated ventilation, and shares a light partition (x 10.25-10.33) with the shower room, with the washing machine pressed against it (machine x 9.65-10.25). Humidity and spin-cycle vibration go straight into the clothes storage. The closet buffers nothing: it sits between the bathroom and an external wall, not between the bedroom and a noisy room. Pattern broken: P13 Closets between rooms (used where it gives no buffer, absent where it would, S16).

**S26 - Bedroom - working at the desk - improvement.** Scenario: sitting; working. The Desk (x 6.55-7.15, y 0.80-2.20) is against the west partition and the Desk_Chair (x 7.25-7.67) faces it. The user looks at a blank wall with the door behind the right shoulder and the 2.4 x 2.4 m south French window (x 8.20-10.60) behind and to the left: a screen facing east picks up the glazing as reflections for most of the day. No desk lamp is registered. Patterns broken: P09 Window place (work surface by the main window) and P14 Pools of light (light at the desk). Method par. 7: "workstation ... with glare-free light".

**S27 - Bedroom - the night trip to the WC - improvement.** Scenario: night route bed-bathroom. The bed (x 10.00-12.20) is at the far east end, and the only door (x 6.72-7.57) is at the far west end. The report's night route is 8.7 m, through two doors and a 90 degree turn in the hall. It starts from the north side, a 65 cm strip (report, below the 70 cm comfort target) closed at the head by Nightstand_2 (y 2.95-3.40). No pattern in P01-P18 applies; rule broken: method par. 8 (night route bed-bathroom, checked here only for continuity, not for length or safety).

**S28 - Bedroom - sleeping next to 5.8 m2 of south glass - improvement.** Scenario: sleeping; opening windows and blinds. The bed's south long side runs 70 cm from the floor-to-ceiling south French window (net x 8.20-10.60, 2.4 m high), and its foot overlaps the window's east panel (x 10.00-10.60). That means summer heat and early light on the sleeper, curtains stacking in the 70 cm bedside strip next to Nightstand_1, and, depending on the floor (unknown in the brief), a bed in full view from outside. Blackout and thermal comfort are NOT VERIFIABLE in the report. Patterns broken: P05 Sleeping to the east (morning light without overheating; the envelope gives only south glazing, which should have been stated) and P12 Bed alcove (bed exposed).

## Walk summary per room

- **Living area (entrance, dining, kitchen, lounge)**: S01-S16. It is the room with the most stories because entrance, dining, kitchen and lounge share one 49.8 m2 space and every route crosses another use zone.
- **Night hall**: S15, S17, S18, S19. Doors open clear of each other (report verified); the problems are length, darkness, the guest path and the hinged cupboard leaf.
- **Bathroom**: S20-S23. The door swing clears the washbasin (leaf tip about 15 cm from it) and the fixtures are reachable (report); the failures are the shower/appliance conflict, ventilation, spacing and laundry.
- **Walk-in closet**: S24, S25. The access zones in front of the units are clear (125 cm, report) and the sliding door is correct for the corridor.
- **Bedroom**: S16, S26, S27, S28. The door leaf parks against the west wall, the bed sides meet the minimum, and floor occupancy is 25%; the failures are desk ergonomics, the night route and the south glazing.
